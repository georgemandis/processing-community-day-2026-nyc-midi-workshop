On the Launchpad

Arrow buttons set the direction. The right column is a speed fader: press higher for faster. The bottom-right button (Stop Solo Mute) pauses and resumes, green while playing and orange while paused; in stock mode it starts and stops the hardware scroll. The logo restarts.
This is the seed for using the board as more than a display.
